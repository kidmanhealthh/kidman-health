Conditions we help with

Remedial massage can support many common aches, stiffness and recovery needs. Everything is delivered at your home, with towels, creams and the massage table provided.

Back pain and stiffness

Lower and upper back tightness, often from sitting, standing, older mattresses or long-term muscle strain.

Neck and shoulder tension

Tightness and restricted movement, including stiffness that builds up through the day or after sleeping awkwardly.

Hip, knee and leg tightness

Muscle tension around the hips, thighs, calves and feet that can make walking and getting up from a chair harder.

Arthritis-related muscle tension

Massage doesn't treat arthritis itself, but gentle work can ease the muscle tightness and aches that often come with stiff joints.

Muscle strains and overuse injuries

Tight or sore muscles from everyday activity, gardening, lifting or exercise.

Tension headaches

Headaches linked to tight neck, jaw and shoulder muscles.

Reduced mobility and deconditioning

Support for muscles that have become stiff from moving less, including after illness or a hospital stay.

Recovery after a fall or injury

Gentle, supportive work as you recover, alongside advice from your GP, physio or other health professionals.

Heavy or puffy limbs

Light lymphatic drainage techniques may suit some clients. Please check with your doctor first, especially with lymphoedema, heart or kidney conditions.

Carer fatigue and stress

Time to rest and release tension for family members and carers who spend their days looking after others.

Please check with your doctor first if you have

  • Blood clots or deep vein thrombosis
  • Fever, infection or contagious illness
  • Recent surgery, fractures or open wounds
  • Lymphoedema, heart or kidney conditions
  • Blood-thinning medication (especially for cupping and dry needling)
  • Cancer treatment or unexplained pain

I don't diagnose conditions. If you have new, severe or unexplained pain, please see your GP. Treatment is tailored to you and adjusted as we go, and I work alongside your doctor, physio or care team.
