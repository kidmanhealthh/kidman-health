Conditions

Conditions we help with

Remedial massage can support many common aches, stiffness and recovery needs. Everything is delivered at your home, with towels, creams and the massage table provided.

Pain & stiffness

Musculoskeletal pain

Aches and tightness in muscles and soft tissue, including the back, neck, shoulders, hips and legs.

Osteoarthritis-related muscle stiffness

Massage doesn't treat arthritis itself, but gentle work can ease the muscle tightness and stiffness that often surrounds sore joints.

Chronic pain conditions

Ongoing pain managed as part of a multi-disciplinary plan, working alongside your GP, physio and care team.

After surgery & mobility

Post-surgical soft tissue restriction

Tightness and restriction in the soft tissues around a surgical site, treated once you have clearance from your surgeon or medical team.

Reduced mobility related to muscle tightness

Muscle tension that makes it harder to walk, get out of a chair or move around the home, including after illness or a hospital stay.

Neurological & movement

Neurological conditions with secondary musculoskeletal impairment

Muscle stiffness and tightness that develops alongside a neurological condition. Treatment is gentle and coordinated with your treating team.

Postural and movement-related muscle dysfunction

Muscle tension linked to posture, limited movement patterns or long periods seated or in bed.

Please check with your doctor first if you have

  • Blood clots or deep vein thrombosis
  • Fever, infection or contagious illness
  • Recent surgery, fractures or open wounds (please get clearance from your surgeon or medical team first)
  • Lymphoedema, heart or kidney conditions
  • Blood-thinning medication (especially for cupping and dry needling)
  • Cancer treatment or unexplained pain

A note on advice

I don't diagnose conditions. If you have new, severe or unexplained pain, please see your GP. Treatment is tailored to you and adjusted as we go, and I work alongside your doctor, physio or care team.

Remedial massage is on the Support at Home and CHSP service lists. Ask your care partner or provider whether it can be included in your plan. See our services.
