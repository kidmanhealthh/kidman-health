All towels, creams and the massage table are provided and brought to your home. See all services.

Who I help in Preston

Older residents living with arthritis-related stiffness or limited mobility, people who spend a lot of time seated, and carers who rarely look after themselves.

Support at Home and CHSP clients

Remedial massage is on the government's service list under therapeutic services for independent living for both Support at Home and the Commonwealth Home Support Programme (CHSP). I work as a subcontracted therapist for aged care providers, so your provider arranges the booking and billing.

Preston is within my close-to-mid service area. To get started, ask your care partner or provider whether remedial massage can be included in your plan and request Kidman Health, or ask them to contact me.

How it works

  1. Check with your provider that remedial massage is part of your approved plan, and ask for Kidman Health.
  2. Your provider or care partner contacts me to confirm availability.
  3. I visit you at home, with everything needed for your session.
  4. I report back to your provider as they require.

Client contributions, session lengths and funding vary by program and provider, so please confirm the details with your provider.

Questions

Can I get remedial massage under Support at Home or CHSP?

If it's included in your approved plan and your provider offers it, yes. Ask your care partner or provider to confirm and to book it with Kidman Health.

Do I need to travel to a clinic?

No. I come to your Preston home, and you can stay in your own chair or bed for treatment.

Can family or carers book for me?

Yes. A family member or carer can make the enquiry, and I'll liaise with your provider.
